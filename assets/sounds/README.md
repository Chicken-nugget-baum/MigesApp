# Sounds

All recordings come from [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com) (Joseph Sardin), licence **CC0 (public domain)**,
cut, mono, loudness-normalised and encoded to MP3 by `ffmpeg`. They are loaded in the background after the first
click (`js/audio.js`, `NB.Audio.loadSamples`); until then, or when the page runs from `file://`, the ZzFX synth
versions are used. The Postauto three-tone horn, the fire-engine siren, the UI click and the villagers' babble stay
synthesized on purpose.
